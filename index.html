About Me

Tech background.
Design heart.

I'm Srihari — a professional transitioning into UI/UX Design from a career in enterprise technology at Wipro Technologies. For over two years, I've worked at the intersection of systems, data, and people, configuring complex benefits administration technology for major corporations like ExxonMobil.

That experience taught me something invaluable: users don't interact with features, they interact with outcomes. Understanding what people actually need — not just what they ask for — is at the core of my design philosophy.

I'm currently completing my Advanced Certification in UI/UX Design with Generative AI from IIITB & Simplilearn, and I'm building a portfolio that reflects real-world thinking and craft.
